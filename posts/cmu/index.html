Looking back at CMU MSML

Looking Back at CMU MSML
December 23, 2025 · Kartik

Note: This post is going to be heavily opinionated

The completion of my 1.5 year master’s degree at CMU MLD calls for a reflection of what CMU had to offer with its MSML program. I have had the pleasure of both TA’ing and taking several of these courses and also serving on its advisory and admissions committee. I would like to share my reviews of the good, the bad and the surprising aspects of this one-of-a-kind master’s in machine learning program.

The most important part of a program is the course material, so let us begin with that. I will state my definition of a “good” course. A good course needs to have the following qualities

  1. The material is interesting and new to the learner and relevant.
  2. The material is well presented to the learner, through well designed slides or well designed lecture notes accompanied with good lecturing.
  3. The assignments are incredibly well designed, difficult and challenging to complete, so that the assignment provides a good learning experience

The only courses that fit this criteria in MSML according to what I have seen are

  1. 10-708 Probabilistic Graphical Models
  2. 10-703 Deep Reinforcement Learning & Control
  • Random
  • Thoughts
  • Cmu
