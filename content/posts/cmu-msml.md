Note: This post is going to be heavily *opinionated* 

The completion of my 1.5 year master's degree at CMU MLD calls for a reflection of what CMU had to offer with its [MSML program](https://ml.cmu.edu/academics/machine-learning-masters-curriculum). I have had the pleasure of both TA'ing and taking several of these courses and also serving on its advisory and admissions committee. I would like to share my reviews of the good, the bad and the surprising aspects of this one-of-a-kind master's in machine learning program.

The most important part of a program is the course material, so let us begin with that. I will state my definition of a "good" course.
A good course needs to have the following qualities 
1. The material is interesting and *new* to the learner and relevant.
2. The material is *well presented* to the learner, through *well designed* slides or *well designed lecture notes*.
3. The assignments are *incredibly well designed*, *difficult* and challenging to complete, so that the assignment provides a *good learning experience*
4. The exams test your understanding of the material in the course and are somewhat correlated with what was taught during the lectures.

In my opinion, all the core courses other than 10-715 and 10-718 fit this criteria.

10-715 has no notes, since the material changes every year, and since its an "intro" much of this course is a random throw of material, and the questions asked in the exam are de-correlated with what was taught in the lectures. 
10-718 has a long explanation that I am going to skip here.


In my second semester, I decided to explore the courses at CMU more, and take more courses from CMU CSD, like [Database systems](https://15445.courses.cs.cmu.edu/spring2026/) and [Computer Systems](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~213/).
This, in hindsight, was the *best* decision I made at CMU. The systems courses at CMU CSD are at least 5 times harder than an average MSML course and far more rewarding

CMU is a great systems and CS school, while it is also a great ML school primarily, the *latter is an outcome of the former*. Maybe ML is too nascent, the courses at CMU MLD are not as hard or as challenging as compared to that in CSD.
