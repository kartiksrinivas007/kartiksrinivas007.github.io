Thanks for checking out my site.

I was a master's student studying computers and math at [CMU MLD](https://www.ml.cmu.edu/). I enjoy working on practical problems -- which means I enjoy nasty heuristics, wrangling with datasets, and debugging model behaviors. I also work on research around safety and unlearning; you can find a few of my papers on the [publications](/publications/) page.

I presently work on developing triton-kernels at [Goomba Lab](https://goombalab.github.io/).

I have also interned on the planner team at [Waymo](https://waymo.com/) where I worked with large autoregressive planning models.

I TA'ed for PGM - [10-708](https://andrejristeski.github.io/10708F25/) at CMU.

I overloaded on systems courses from CSD during Spring 2025 at CMU, taking 5 courses and simultaneously TA'ing for [10-725](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~siva/teaching/725/). I had a 4.1 GPA; some of the courses were Advanced Statistical Theory, PGM, and Database Systems.

Before CMU, I graduated with honors from [Computer Science and Engineering](https://cse.iith.ac.in/) at [IIT Hyderabad](https://www.iith.ac.in/). I completed an additional degree in [Engineering Sciences](https://es.iith.ac.in/), and a minor in [Artificial Intelligence](https://ai.iith.ac.in/index.html), and received the Institute Silver Medal.

I also enjoy mathematical physics. At IITH I took courses in fluid mechanics, electrodynamics, and classical mechanics — and had a lot of fun with all of them.
